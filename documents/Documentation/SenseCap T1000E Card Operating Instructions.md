# SenseCap T1000E Card Operating Instructions

When you first power on after the update, the Card will come up in Default configuration.
Which is operaring in **FANET mode**, and the aircraft type is preset to **Paraglider**.  
The solid green LED means the battery power is good, and the device is **NOT airborne**.

    <span>
        <strong>Battery Charge Status Beeps</strong><br>
        After switching the device on, the battery charge status is indicated with a sequence of short beeps:<br>
        • <strong>5x Beep</strong> means that the battery is charged 95% or more.<br>
        • <strong>4x Beep</strong> means that the battery is charged 75% or more.<br>
        • <strong>3x Beep</strong> means that the battery is charged 55% or more.<br>
        • <strong>2x Beep</strong> means that the battery is charged 35% or more.<br>
        • <strong>1x Beep</strong> means that the battery is charged 15% or more.<br>
        When the battery is less than 15% charged, you will hear a constant beep for one second after switching the device on.<br>
        The <strong>LOW</strong> battery charge level is also indicated by rapid flashing LED.
    </span>
